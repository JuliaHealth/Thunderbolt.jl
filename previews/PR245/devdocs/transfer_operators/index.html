Thunderbolt.WendlandRadialBasisFunction — Type
WendlandRadialBasisFunction{Dim, k}

A compactly supported Wendland radial basis function with spatial dimension Dim and smoothness degree 2k.

For the three-dimensional case used in Thunderbolt, the kernel is defined by

\[\phi(r) = \begin{cases} (1 - r)_+^2, & k = 0, \\ (1 - r)_+^4 (1 + 4 r), & k = 1, \\ (1 - r)_+^6 (35 r^2 + 18 r + 3), & k = 2. \end{cases}\]

Here $(x)_+ = \max(x, 0)$ and $r$ is the normalized distance.

Thunderbolt.IntergridDofMapping — Type
IntergridDofMapping{VecT, IntT}

A mapping between nodes and degrees of freedom used for field transfer.

The mapping stores the coordinates of the selected source and destination dofs and provides fast translations between dof indices and node indices for transfer evaluation.

source
Thunderbolt.EuclideanDistanceMeasureCache — Type
EuclideanDistanceMeasureCache(M, α)

Cache for Euclidean distance computations used in RBF transfer evaluation.

M and α are the number of connected neighbors used for support radius calculation and the scaling factor for support radii.
